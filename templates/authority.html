Add Member Directly

Members can also self-register at /register using your Room ID above and wait for your approval there. This form adds and activates someone directly into your room without them registering themselves.

Set one yourself, or leave it blank and one will be generated and shown to you once — either way, you must relay it to the member yourself; they are not emailed or texted automatically.

Authority Rule

Immutable Administration

  • Only Higher Authority can grant or revoke access.
  • Every grant/revoke/role event gets a permanent event ID and hash-chain position.
  • No audit or access event can be edited or deleted.
  • Authority profile views and privileged actions are themselves logged.
  • Evidence deletion is authority-only and always triggers mandatory notifications.
