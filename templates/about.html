Important limitations

Forensic correctness

  • Hash values are fingerprints; they are not decryptable plaintext.
  • Dictionary matching tests candidates from a supplied wordlist.
  • Hash identification is probabilistic; length alone cannot prove an algorithm.
  • The current app copies uploaded evidence into its case directory and is a research prototype.
  • An integrity mismatch indicates changed or missing content; it does not establish who changed it or why.
